Questions

Frequently asked questions

◆
Do I need a consultation before treatment?

Yes, for all medical treatments. Your doctor will assess your skin, discuss your goals and medical history, and recommend a plan. Spa services can be booked directly.

Is there any downtime?

Many treatments, such as Hydrafacials and spa rituals, have none. Others, like peels, microneedling or fractional laser, may cause redness for a few days. We'll explain exactly what to expect beforehand.

Who performs the treatments?

Injectables and laser treatments are performed by licensed doctors or trained nurses under medical supervision. Facials and spa services are delivered by certified therapists.

How should I prepare for my appointment?

Arrive with clean skin if possible, and let us know about any medications, allergies or recent sun exposure. We'll send specific instructions when you book.

What is your cancellation policy?

We kindly ask for at least 24 hours’ notice to reschedule or cancel, so we can offer the time to another client.

Which payment methods do you accept?

We accept cash, major credit and debit cards, and popular e-wallets. Package payments can be discussed at reception.
